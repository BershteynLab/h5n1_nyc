Parameter table

Exhaustive inventory of the NYC H5N1 model. Maps and the scenario comparison use the scenario midpoint. Where a literature range exists it is shown in brackets after the midpoint (e.g. 1.25 [1.2, 1.4]); that is a literature bound from parameters.csv, not a statistical confidence interval. Shiny sliders explore around the midpoint and do not change the static HTML results until the maps are re-run. Open the Shiny app to vary slider parameters.

In presented results

  • Yes Used in the maps and tables we presented.
  • In code, off The model can use it, but we set it to zero (so it does not change results).
